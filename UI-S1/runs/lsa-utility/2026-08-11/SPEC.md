# Utility-Aligned Learned Structural Aggregator

Date: 2026-08-11

Status: `FROZEN_BEFORE_UTILITY_RESULTS`

Workdir: `runs/lsa-utility/2026-08-11/`

## 1. Motivation

The previous LSA optimized candidate correctness. Candidate AUROC was high, yet direct top-1 selection was worse than CEV-A. The training target therefore did not match the deployed decision: whether a candidate should override the strong CEV-A fallback.

This study trains the relative utility of an override. It combines two verified ideas:

1. GTA1/GRPO: for each prompt, sample a group, reward evaluator-aligned successful clicks, and normalize reward within the group. GTA1 uses eight generations, `reward_funcs=accuracy`, `beta=0`; its trainer computes `(reward - group_mean)/(group_std + 1e-5)`. The GTA1 report explicitly notes that all-correct/all-incorrect batches lose reward signal.
2. MVP: form deterministic complete-link geometric groups; cluster size is primary and AGVP coverage only breaks ties; return a real member. MVP is an inference rule, not a GRPO-trained aggregator.

We use GTA-style group-relative utility as a training target and MVP-style support/reliability as structural state features. This is not on-policy GRPO because the candidate banks are fixed and generated by heterogeneous policies. The correct term is `GRPO-inspired offline group-relative utility learning`.

## 2. Scope and disclosure

All previous CEV and LSA outcomes are known. This is a new exploratory training objective, not independent confirmation. It evaluates all four arms jointly with row-grouped outer folds. No arm or benchmark may be omitted.

No new VLM inference or GPU training is used.

## 3. Exact fallback

For every benchmark, arm, and row, reconstruct the frozen CEV-A candidate using that row's own upstream outer fold, whose source reliability is fitted on the other four folds. Use that fold's original configuration, scale, and threshold. The reconstructed candidate correctness must match the stored CEV-A output row by row and remain invariant across Utility-LSA inner splits.

This exact CEV candidate is the reference action $b_r$.

## 4. Utility rewards

For candidate $i$ in row/arm group $r$:

$$
u_{ri}=R_{ri}-R_{rb}\in\{-1,0,+1\},
$$

where $R$ is frozen evaluator Step Success and $b$ is the exact CEV-A candidate.

- `+1`: candidate repairs a CEV failure;
- `0`: candidate leaves final correctness unchanged;
- `-1`: candidate breaks a CEV success.

Groups with constant utility contain no override information and do not train the utility model. They remain in evaluation.

### U-RAW

Regression target is $u_{ri}$.

### U-GRPO

Regression target is the GTA-style group-relative advantage:

$$
A_{ri}=\frac{u_{ri}-\bar u_r}{\sigma(u_r)+10^{-4}}.
$$

### U-HYBRID

Regression target is $0.5u_{ri}+0.5A_{ri}$. This tests whether absolute deployment utility and within-group contrast are complementary.

The inner OOF procedure selects among U-RAW, U-GRPO, and U-HYBRID. No objective is added after results.

## 5. Features

Use the frozen no-action LSA feature set. For each candidate, construct:

$$
z_i=[x_i, x_b, x_i-x_b, |x_i-x_b|],
$$

where $x_b$ is the exact fallback candidate feature vector.

The features include MVP-inspired geometric support, cluster-support proxies at fixed radii, distinct-lineage support, dispersion, parameter agreement, stage, parse state, and cross-fitted reliability. Action one-hot, benchmark ID, source/model ID, raw slot ID, instruction/raw-response hashes, screenshots, and test GT are prohibited.

MVP AGVP coverage is not available on both benchmarks and is not fabricated. Cross-fitted reliability is only a generic tie-quality feature, not called AGVP coverage.

## 6. Training groups and weights

Training pools all four arms but keeps the same underlying row entirely within one fold. Constant-utility arm-groups are excluded. Each underlying row with at least one active arm has fixed total weight, split equally across its active arms and then equally across 12 candidates. Each benchmark is normalized to equal total mass.

Rows shared across arms never cross train/test boundaries. Exact duplicate candidate feature/utility pairs within an underlying row may be retained but their total row weight remains fixed, preventing repeated candidates from increasing row influence.

## 7. Estimator grid

CPU sklearn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, squared-error loss:

| ID | Learning rate | Leaves | Min leaf | L2 | Iterations |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| H1 | 0.05 | 7 | 50 | 10 | 100 |
| H2 | 0.05 | 15 | 50 | 10 | 100 |
| H3 | 0.10 | 15 | 20 | 1 | 100 |

Cross product with U-RAW/U-GRPO/U-HYBRID gives nine frozen training configurations.

## 8. Safe policy

Let $s_i$ be predicted utility and $s_b$ the fallback score. Let $c_L=\arg\max_i s_i$. Override only when:

$$
c_L\ne b,\qquad s_{c_L}-s_b\ge\tau,\qquad s_{c_L}>0.
$$

Threshold candidates are infinity, zero, and positive-margin deciles from OOF predictions.

Inner selection eligibility:

- each benchmark equal-arm OOF loss is no worse than `-0.25 MDE`;
- no individual arm × benchmark cell loses more than `0.5 MDE`.

Among eligible objective/model/threshold triples, maximize the equal-benchmark/equal-arm standardized Step-SR delta versus CEV-A. Ties prefer larger threshold, then U-GRPO, U-HYBRID, U-RAW, then H1–H3.

## 9. Nested protocol

For outer fold $k$:

1. Hold out fold $k$ for all four arms and both benchmarks.
2. On the other four folds, produce four-fold OOF utility predictions; each OOF model trains on the other three folds, all arms, both benchmarks.
3. Select objective, HGB configuration, and one global threshold using OOF final Step SR under the safety constraints.
4. Refit on all four outer-development folds.
5. Evaluate once on all eight outer-test cells.

No target-test label enters feature construction, reliability, objective selection, or threshold selection.

## 10. Required controls

- frozen CEV-A;
- frozen nested dev-selection;
- previous correctness-trained LSA safe outputs;
- utility direct top-1 without safety threshold;
- U-RAW, U-GRPO, U-HYBRID OOF selections and held-out usage counts;
- no-MVP-structure ablation: remove coordinate support, medoid distance, lineage support, and dispersion from $x$;
- absolute-only ablation: use $x_i$ without fallback-pair features.

## 11. Statistics and gates

Use 10,000 paired grouped bootstrap replicates and 99% percentile intervals. Report all arm × benchmark cells and equal-arm/equal-benchmark standardized means.

### UR1: safety

Every arm × benchmark cell is non-inferior to CEV-A: CI upper bound nonnegative or absolute loss below MDE.

### UR2: robust Mind2Web gain

Mind2Web equal-arm mean utility-safe minus CEV-A has 99% CI lower bound above zero.

### UR3: ScreenSpot preservation

ScreenSpot equal-arm mean is non-inferior and any point loss is below MDE.

### UR4: stronger than dev-selection

Equal-benchmark/equal-arm standardized mean versus nested dev-selection has 99% CI lower bound above zero, with no individual cell losing more than MDE.

### UR5: target alignment

Utility-safe must outperform correctness-trained LSA-safe in equal-benchmark/equal-arm standardized paired bootstrap, or achieve UR2 when correctness-LSA did not.

## 12. Kill conditions

| ID | Trigger | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UR-K1 | Exact CEV candidate reconstruction mismatch | Stop and debug |
| UR-K2 | Any cell fails UR1 | Utility model unsafe; stop learned line |
| UR-K3 | Infinity threshold selected in at least 3/5 folds | No reproducible override policy |
| UR-K4 | U-GRPO is selected in 0/5 folds and is worse than U-RAW in OOF objective in at least 4/5 folds | GTA-style normalization not useful for aggregation |
| UR-K5 | No-MVP-structure is not worse and structure permutation importance is negligible | MVP structure is not the source of learned signal |

Only UR-K1 permits debugging. Other kills do not authorize changing rewards, features, or model grids.

## 13. Interpretation

- If UR1–UR5 pass: utility-aligned safe aggregator becomes the learned method candidate, still requiring a new independent benchmark for final confirmation.
- If only UR1/UR3 pass: safe exploratory override, appendix only.
- If UR2 passes but UR4 fails: improvement over CEV-A but not over benchmark-specific method selection.
- If UR1 fails: retain CEV-A as strongest aggregator and close learned training on current data.
